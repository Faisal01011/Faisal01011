## Featured Builds

### 🎮 [IONSTORM](https://github.com/Faisal01011/IONSTORM) — Neon WebGPU Arcade Shooter
**Defend the Veil. Ride the Surge.** A replayable browser shooter with escalating enemy waves, four-phase Dreadnought bosses with rotating attack doctrines, in-run XP drafts, and UTC-seeded daily challenges. Renders on **WebGPU** with an automatic **WebGL2 fallback**, ships as an installable offline-first **PWA**, and syncs progression through optional Supabase accounts.

`WebGPU` `WebGL2` `Service Workers` `Supabase` `CI (GitHub Actions)`

**[▶ Play it live](https://ionstorm.vercel.app)** · ⭐ Starred repo

---

### 🌍 [Wild Roads](https://github.com/Faisal01011/wild-roads) — Procedural Open World
A chunk-streamed 3D world built in **Three.js + TypeScript**, featuring a bicycle-model vehicle physics simulation, predator-prey animal AI driven by finite-state machines (deer flee, wolves hunt), instanced grass rendered with custom shaders, and a full power-up system. Includes a switchable player mode — pilot a car or slither as a snake through the same terrain.

`Three.js` `TypeScript` `Custom Shaders (GLSL)` `Procedural Generation` `FSM AI`

**[▶ Play it live](https://wild-roads.vercel.app)**

---

### 🎙️ [Signal](https://github.com/Faisal01011/signal-speech-to-text) — Self-Hosted Speech-to-Text
A real transcription app, not a wrapper around a free cloud API. Runs **faster-whisper** (CTranslate2, int8-quantized) entirely on CPU for word-level timestamps and per-word confidence scoring, with browser-native audio capture via `MediaRecorder` and Postgres-backed history — built specifically to survive redeploys, unlike the typical `PyAudio` + SQLite demo.

`FastAPI` `faster-whisper` `React + Vite` `Docker` `Supabase Postgres`

**[▶ Try it live](https://signal-speech-to-text.vercel.app)**

---

### 🧠 [EcoMind](https://github.com/Faisal01011/ecomind) — Voice-First Memory App
A voice-memory capture app pairing **FastAPI + Faster-Whisper** transcription on the backend with **Ollama**-powered semantic recall and a **React** frontend — exploring what a private, offline-capable AI memoir tool could look like.

`FastAPI` `Faster-Whisper` `Ollama` `React`
